XNotch 0.8.0 puts a notch on your other displays, renames the shelf to the Tray, and tidies the pills when you have lots of extensions.

- Show on all displays, in Settings → General → Displays, gives every connected display its own notch. They share one tray, one player and the same extensions, so a file dropped on any of them lands in the same tray.
- A display without a camera notch gets one drawn at the top. Drawn notch height makes it as tall as the menu bar, or as tall as a real notch.
- Alerts, headphone toasts and files sent from extensions show on the display your pointer is on. Displays can be plugged in and unplugged while XNotch runs.
- The Display picker chooses which display the notch lives on. Automatic uses the built-in notch, or else the display with the menu bar, and a display that isn't connected hands the notch back to Automatic.
- The notch's shelf is now called the Tray, so it's never mixed up with the floating shelves from the Shelves extension. Everything in it stays where it was.
- With four or more extensions on, the pills under the notch show only their icons, so the row doesn't get crowded. If you've picked a style in Settings → General, your choice stays.

The extra notches are off until you switch on Show on all displays.
