XNotch 0.16.0 redesigns Walkie's conversations and settings, makes copy and paste work everywhere, and brings a new icon.

- Walkie: conversations in the notch now fit the notch. The face you open flies up into the header, the composer is one line, and holding the mic records right in the conversation while your words appear as you speak.
- Walkie: the menu bar conversation scrolls back through older messages, and glides smoothly to each new one.
- Walkie: unread messages show as a red dot on the person's face in the menu bar.
- Walkie: simpler settings. Copy or share your invite, paste a friend's to add them straight away, and accept or decline friend requests in the new Requests list. Without a server, Walkie says so first and shows what to fill in.
- Walkie: right-click any message to copy it.
- Copy, paste, select all and undo now work with the keyboard in every XNotch text field, including Settings.
- Notes: the show and hide shortcut can use a modifier on the right side of the keyboard, like right ⌘ with period, leaving the left one free for other apps.
- AI Usage: explains why a provider can't be read, such as being offline or rate-limited, and tries again when the network is back.
- Shelves: Delete and other shelf shortcuts stay with the shelf you clicked, and the selection dims while another app has the keyboard.
- Menu Bar Folders: XNotch's own icons, like Walkie's faces, are never hidden behind the folders.
- New silver icon for the Mac app and the iPhone and iPad companion.
- iPhone and iPad companion: a light redesign with an iPad layout, and USB connections recover and reconnect faster.
