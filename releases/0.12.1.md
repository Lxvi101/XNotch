XNotch 0.12.1 makes images dragged from ChatGPT in Zen and Firefox land on shelves.

- Images from ChatGPT conversations in Zen and Firefox now land on the shelf. In 0.12.0 the shelf took the drop but stayed empty.
- ChatGPT's generated images, dragged at full size, open a shelf when you shake them and land as the original PNG, named as ChatGPT names them.
