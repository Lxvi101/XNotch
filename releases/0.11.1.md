XNotch 0.11.1 brings an extension store, makes Settings lists draggable again, and adds line editing and Send to Tray to Notes.

- Settings → Extensions is now a store: every extension as a card with a preview, and a page for each with its details. Get adds one, Remove takes it out, and the sidebar lists only the ones you've added.
- Pages in Settings → General and the tiles in Vitals' settings can be dragged to reorder again. Drag a page below Hidden to take it out of the notch.
- In a note, ⌥↑ and ⌥↓ move the current lines up and down, and ⌥⇧↑ and ⌥⇧↓ copy them.
- ⌘⌫ on a line with nothing left before the cursor removes the line.
- Right-click a file in a note's bucket, or a picture in the note, to send it to the tray.
- Dragging a file out of a note's bucket is smooth again, and it no longer drops back into the same note.
