XNotch 0.7.0 adds Converter, a wheel that opens under a file you're dragging and converts it or works on it in place, and lets shelves merge.

- New extension, Converter. Hold ⌘ while dragging a file, pause or shake, and a wheel of formats opens under the pointer. Drop on a format to convert, on the outer ring for a tool, or in the middle to cancel. The result is saved next to the original, which is never changed.
- Converts images (including HEIC, WebP, AVIF and camera RAW), GIFs, PDFs, documents, video and audio. PDFs turn into one image per page, and videos that are already H.264 or HEVC are repackaged as MP4 or MOV without re-encoding.
- Tools: Cut Out removes an image's background, Compress and Half Size work on images, PDFs and video, Strip Info removes location and camera data, Copy Text reads text from images, PDFs and documents, and Mute removes a video's sound.
- Settings choose the key (⌘, ⇧, ⌥ or ⌃) and whether it opens the wheel when you pause, when you shake, or at once. Dragging without the key leaves everything alone, and Shelves ignores shakes made with the wheel's key held.
- Shelves whose files are all one kind get Convert in their menu, and the notch shelf gets a header button. The wheel opens at the pointer, works on every file at once, and the results land back on the same shelf.
- Video, audio, WebP and AVIF use ffmpeg. Converter uses the one already on your Mac, from Homebrew, MacPorts or your shell. Without one, Settings has an Install button that downloads a signed build, or dropping on a format that needs it installs it first. Nothing is downloaded until you ask.
- Drag a shelf over another one to merge them. Files already on the target shelf are skipped.
- Extensions can offer actions on shelf files and hand new files back to the shelf, and can claim a key for gestures made while dragging (XNotchKit 1.6.0).

Converter and shelf merging are new, so please report anything that behaves oddly. Extensions are switched off until you add them in Settings → Extensions.
