XNotch 0.6.1 keeps every page in reach and tidies up Settings.

- With more than four pages, the page switcher continues on the right of the camera instead of running under it. The highlight slides behind the camera from one side to the other.
- While it does, the shelf's buttons fold into one ⋯ menu with the same actions: Cloud Links, sending to your iPhone or iPad, floating shelves, AirDrop and Clear Shelf.
- Settings: page titles no longer overlap content scrolled beneath them, and the sidebar reaches the bottom of the window.
