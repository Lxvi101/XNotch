XNotch 0.12.2 shows how much of your weekly AI limit you can use today, and takes deleted files off shelves from watched folders.

- AI Usage can show today's share of a weekly limit: the week split into seven days, counted from the limit's reset. A tick on the Weekly bar marks how far you can go today, and the details show what's left. Claude and other tiles with a free lane also show it as "Today left". Turn it on in Settings → AI Usage → Display.
- Files deleted from a watched folder, or moved to the Trash, now leave their shelf, and the shelf closes once it's empty. Pinned shelves stay open.
- Files moved to the Trash no longer come back when a shelf is reopened.
