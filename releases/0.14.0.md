XNotch 0.14.0 adds nested spaces to Notes, CLIProxyAPI hubs to AI Usage, and post-quantum encryption to Walkie.

- Notes: deleting a note or space keeps its windows open instead of closing them.
- AI Usage: connect to a CLIProxyAPI hub to see Claude and Codex usage through it.
- Walkie: messages now use post-quantum encryption, and the menu bar has been refreshed.
