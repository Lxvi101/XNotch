XNotch 0.15.0 adds Menu Bar Folders, a permission checkup, and faster note creation in Notes.

- Menu Bar Folders: a new extension that tucks menu bar icons into folders, with a drawer that opens under the menu bar when you hover a folder.
- XNotch now checks for permissions an extension needs when you turn it on, and walks you through granting them.
- Notes: in the switcher, ⌘↩ creates a new note in the space you're looking at, and ↩ does too when the space is empty or nothing matches your search. A typed search becomes the new note's title.
- Notes: ⌥↩ or ⌥-click opens a note from the switcher in a new window (previously ⌘).
- Notes: the switcher no longer gets cut off in narrow note windows.
- Notes: removed the space button next to the note title, which did the same as the switcher button.
