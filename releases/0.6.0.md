XNotch now shows how your Mac is doing, floats shelves wherever you drag files, and says hello to your headphones.

- Vitals: CPU, memory and network (or GPU, disk and battery) as tiles in the notch, each one its own live graph. Pick and order up to four in Settings → Extensions → Vitals.
- Click CPU or Memory and the tile opens into the apps behind the number, helpers counted with their app. Click an app to see its processes, or right-click to quit it.
- Alerts beside the camera when your disk runs low, memory pressure rises, the battery gets low, your Mac slows down to cool off, or the CPU stays busy. Each one can be switched off, tuned and previewed.
- Shelves: shake the pointer while dragging files, or hold a key, and a shelf appears to hold them. Drag them out later, or drop them on AirDrop, Messages, Mail and more. Watched folders put new files on a shelf as they arrive.
- AirPods, Beats and other Bluetooth headphones appear in the notch when they connect, with their battery. Turn it off in Settings → Music.
- Cloud Links can carry a password: use "Link with Password…" on the shelf or in a file's menu. Needs the latest XNotch Cloud server.
- Vitals and Shelves are extensions and start switched off. Add them under Settings → Extensions.

Extensions can now show alerts in the notch (XNotchKit 1.4.0). Extensions built for 1.3.0 keep working.
