XNotch 0.17.0 brings live talk to Walkie, message banners in the notch, and quiet during Focus.

- Walkie: your menu bar is now the people you work with right now. In Settings, drag faces between "In your menu bar" and "Everyone else".
- Walkie: talk live. When someone has you in their menu bar too, their face says Live: hold ⌃⌥Space from any app, or hold their face, and they hear you as you speak. Everyone else gets voice notes as before. Choose other keys in Settings.
- Walkie: new messages show as a banner in the notch with the person's picture, their name and what they wrote, like a message on iPhone. Clicking it opens that conversation.
- Walkie: while a Focus such as Do Not Disturb is on, nothing rings, pops up or plays by itself. Unread dots still show. Live talk from people in your menu bar still comes through.
- Walkie: click a file or screenshot in a conversation to preview it with Quick Look, double-click to open it, or drag it anywhere to take a copy. Right-click for Show in Finder, Copy and Save to Downloads.
- Walkie: a picture sent on its own shows large in the menu bar conversation.
- Extensions: new Kit API 1.11.0 for message banners, Focus and alert clicks.

Seeing Focus needs Full Disk Access, which XNotch asks for during setup. Live talk works once both of you are on 0.17.0.
