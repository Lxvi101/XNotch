XNotch 0.6.4 improves file shelves and quick drops, adds a quieter music control and experiments with expressive Vitals alerts.

- Drag files out of a Dock stack, such as Downloads, and shake the pointer to open a shelf.
- Shelves now toggle Quick Look with Space, close it with Space or Escape, and update the preview when you select another file. Keyboard focus returns to the shelf when the preview closes.
- Shelves prepare thumbnails before showing files where possible, reducing the change from generic icons to previews.
- Dropping from Photos uses the promised original file instead of the small preview inside its library.
- Music settings let you replace the moving visualizer beside the camera with a still play/pause symbol. Click it to play or pause; the open player keeps its visualizer.
- Vitals alert settings offer an experimental warning sign with animated notch eyes: worried for warnings and shocked for critical alerts.
- Quick drops onto the notch, including AirDrop, keep the drop zone open until the files arrive.
- During Full Disk Access setup, click the notch face to return to the guide and choose Not Now.
