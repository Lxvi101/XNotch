XNotch now introduces itself, asks for the access it needs, and has eyes.

- A welcome tour on first launch: the screen dims, the notch flies into place and opens into a short tour of the shelf, music and destination pills.
- Full Disk Access by default. XNotch opens the right pane in System Settings and docks a guide beside its window; drag XNotch from the guide into the list, and the guide flies back into the notch once access is on.
- Eyes in the notch that follow your pointer and watch the permission guide.
- What's New in Settings, with the changes in every version.
- Permissions in Settings → General, plus a button to replay the welcome tour.
- On first launch the shelf, Now Playing and extensions wait for the Full Disk Access step, so macOS no longer asks for folders one at a time.

Keep XNotch in Applications before granting Full Disk Access: from Downloads or Documents, macOS asks about that folder first. macOS applies Full Disk Access once XNotch restarts; the guide offers a Relaunch button.

This release is Developer ID signed but not notarized. macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security on first launch.
