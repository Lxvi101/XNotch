XNotch 0.11.0 adds Drives, lets you hide pages from the notch, and lets notes show pictures from their bucket.

- Drives, a new extension, lists USB sticks, SD cards and external disks with their free space, announces new ones in the notch, and ejects them in one click. If an app is holding a disk, it tells you which.
- Pages can be hidden: in Settings → General → Pages, drag one below Hidden to take it out of the pills and the open notch.
- In Notes, type @ to put a picture from the note's bucket into the text. On its own line it shows as a preview; among words, as a small rounded chip. Right-click it to make it inline, medium, large or full width.
- Double-click a picture in a note to see it large, right beside the note.
- Notes now fade in and out where they are, with their text coming into focus, instead of flying out of the notch.
- Opening and closing a note's bucket keeps your place in the note.
- The show and hide shortcut brings back your latest note when all of them are closed, rather than starting a new one.
- The Settings sidebar always stays open.

Drives is off until you add it in Settings → Extensions.
