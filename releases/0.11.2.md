XNotch 0.11.2 puts dropped files into a note's text and fixes a note that stopped taking typing after a drag from its bucket.

- Files dropped on a note go into its bucket and into the text where you drop them, and the cursor follows the drag so you can see where. Files dropped elsewhere on the note go in at the cursor.
- Pasting files into a note writes them in at the cursor, as well as keeping them in the bucket.
- Dragging a file from a note's bucket onto its text writes it in there. The bucket folds away and you can keep typing. Before, the note stopped taking typing until it was hidden and shown again.
- ⌘N starts a new note in the same window, and ⇧⌘N a new floating one.
