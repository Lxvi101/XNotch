XNotch now reaches your iPhone and iPad, and shares files as links from your own server.

- iPhone & iPad: share files from any app on your iPhone or iPad, and they land on the shelf, saved to Downloads. Text goes straight to the clipboard. Send shelf files back with the phone button or a file's right-click menu while the XNotch app is open on the phone.
- It uses the fastest route that's up: a USB cable, Wi-Fi (or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi when you're near), the internet through your own server, or Bluetooth. Everything is end-to-end encrypted with the key from the pairing code.
- Cloud Links: click the cloud on the shelf to upload files to your own XNotch Cloud server and copy a link that expires after an hour, a day, a week or a month. Settings → Extensions → Cloud Links lists your links and can delete them early.
- XNotch Cloud, the server for links and the internet route, is in the repository's Server folder: one small program for a VPS or a Raspberry Pi, deployable with Docker or Dokploy.
- Both are extensions and start switched off. Add them in the welcome tour or under Settings → Extensions.
- The XNotch app for iPhone and iPad is built from the repository's Companion folder with Xcode.

Extensions built for earlier versions need rebuilding against XNotchKit 1.3.0 (ABI 3).
